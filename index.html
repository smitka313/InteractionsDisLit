A quick guide

  1. Find your lesson. Choose a unit, then an investigation and activity. A selected activity opens automatically.
  2. Find a literacy task. Search keywords or choose a strategy. Searches match all words, in any order. Strategy buttons match any selected strategy.
  3. Open an activity. Each numbered entry explains what students do and shows its PDF page reference. An amber badge marks an assigned reading whose full text is not in the source guide.
  4. Keep your place. Use ☆ to save activities on this browser. “Link” copies a direct activity link, and the page remembers your filters when browser storage is available.
  5. Plan with your team. Print the matching activities or export the visible entries as a CSV file. Printing a single activity is available inside each open activity.

Opening the exact source page

Use Source PDFs to choose your original investigation PDFs. Page buttons then open the matching PDF at the first cited page. Files stay in this browser session; they are not uploaded. You will need to select the PDFs again after reopening the dashboard.

Useful searches

Choosing a unit, investigation, or activity clears search, strategy, and saved-only filters so the chosen lessons are visible. Copying a local-file link is useful on the same computer; share a hosted dashboard URL with colleagues.

Connect your source PDFs

Choose the investigation PDFs you uploaded. The dashboard matches them by unit and investigation, including filenames with extra copy numbers. They stay on your device and are available for this session.

    Page references use PDF page positions starting at 1, not the smaller activity-page labels. Your browser’s PDF viewer determines whether an exact page jump is supported. A different edition may have different pagination.

    Coverage & source notes

    What was included

    This catalog maps 251 grouped literacy entries to all 49 activities in the 13 unique uploaded investigation guides (819 PDF pages). It includes student prompts and teacher-directed routines for scientific reading, writing, discussion, evidence use, representations, data, vocabulary, questions, and reflection.

    Related questions within a task are grouped into one entry. Counts represent these grouped entries, not individual questions or class periods. Tags describe the literacy work required by a documented task; they do not mean the curriculum uses that strategy name.

    Boundaries of the review

    Assigned readings and worksheets that are only named or linked in the guides are marked “Referenced reading.” Their full texts, separate appendices, and live online activities were not audited. The catalog uses the supplied PDFs and should not be treated as a guarantee that every possible literacy interpretation or later curriculum revision is included. NGSS alignment statements and teacher-only background reading are not counted as student literacy tasks.

    Versions and corrections

    • The second copy of Unit 1 Investigation 3 was identical and was counted once.
    • Activity titles follow the uploaded guides. Unit 2 Investigation 3 titles differ from the curriculum overview; the earlier titles are searchable aliases.
    • The 2018–2019 Unit 4 Investigation 2 contains three activities and replaces the overview’s older two-activity sequence.
    • Unit 4 Investigation 1, p. 63: question 11 labels “hydrophilic” as water-fearing. The intended term is hydrophobic. IUPAC terminology ↗
    • Unit 4 Investigation 2, p. 63: the question describing hypothetical vaccines as containing antibodies is an inaccurate vaccine-ingredient description. This catalog summarizes the comparison of binding models and flags the source wording. CDC explanation ↗
